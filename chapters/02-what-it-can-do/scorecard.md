# My can / can't scorecard

My surface (Cowork or Claude Code):

|  |  |
|  |  |
|  |  |

## Seeds the book suggests

Left column: draft my invoices, chase overdue payments, prep my books, write customer replies, produce marketing.

Right column, in your own handwriting: sign my tax return, make the final call on money, replace my accountant or my judgment.

## The book's example: the pool company

| Claude can do this for my business | Claude can't, or shouldn't, do this alone |
|---|---|
| Draft my invoices | File my taxes or give tax advice |
| Chase overdue payments | Make the final call on a refund or a price change |
| Prep the month-end packet for my accountant | Sign a contract or a service estimate |
| Draft customer replies in my voice | Email my biggest account without my review |
| Build my marketing posts and campaigns | Replace my accountant or my own judgment |
