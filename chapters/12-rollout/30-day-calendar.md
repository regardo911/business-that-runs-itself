## If a full week is more than your life allows

- Week 1 minimum: context pack + one read-only brief.
- Week 2 minimum: one verified connector + one invoice or reminder draft.
- Week 3 minimum: one real customer reply + one follow-up + one named custom-workflow gap to build next.
- Week 4 minimum: a guardrails policy + one subscription canceled.

## If you miss a week

Run the last read-only workflow you trust to get your bearings, check whether your context pack is still current, and resume at the next unfinished deliverable.
