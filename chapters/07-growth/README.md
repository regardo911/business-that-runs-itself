# Chapter 7: growth

What you fill in is `four-week-campaign.md`: four weeks of posts, one row each. Run each row through the five questions under the table before you approve it.

## What you run in Cowork

The three things the book has you do this week:

- `/call-list`, or "who am I calling today?" Make at least one of the calls.
- One real customer message: say "a customer is upset" or "draft a response" and paste it in. The book's `/handle-complaint` is gone. `ticket-deflector` takes it now and waits for your yes on any refund.
- One marketing asset: "what should I promote?" (`content-strategy` writes the brief), then "run this brief" (`canva-creator` makes the Canva posts). The book's `/run-campaign` is gone; those two do its two halves.

If a draft sounds generic, add two or three more real messages to `brand-voice-and-numbers.md` and ask again.

## Done when

> "You handled one real customer inquiry end to end (logged, drafted, sent or scheduled) and produced one marketing asset you'd actually publish, with your approval on everything that reached a customer."
