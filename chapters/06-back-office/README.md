# Chapter 6: the back office

Type `/plan-payroll` (or "can I make payroll?"), then `/close-month`. Check the cash number against QuickBooks. Read every reminder draft before you approve it.

`month-end-packet.md` is the outline of what your accountant gets. Copy it once a month, fill it from what `/close-month` hands back, and put the charges it couldn't place under section 4. Your accountant reviews it. The packet only gathers.

You're done when, in the book's words, "You generated a real invoice and drafted a real expense or month-end report, you reviewed both before anything was sent or posted, and the numbers matched your reality."
