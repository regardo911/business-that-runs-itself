# Chapter 9: the teardown sheet

Open `teardown.csv` in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. It has the book's nine columns, eight empty rows and a Total row.

1. One row per recurring software charge from your last two or three statements. Cowork can list them: "list all my recurring software subscriptions and what each costs."
2. Mark each one keep, cut or downgrade. Keep the tools Claude connects to (QuickBooks, Canva, HubSpot, your payment processor).
3. Add a row for your Claude plan, as a cost.
4. Total both cost columns. The difference is your monthly number; times twelve is your year.
5. Cancel one thing this week.

Leave "Cancel/downgrade by" empty until the replacing workflow has done that tool's job for one real cycle and you've checked the result. That's the book's rule, and the date is yours to write.

Chapter 9's finish line: "If you can say 'I'm saving roughly $X a month, net,' and you've canceled at least one subscription to prove it, this chapter delivered."

## The book's example

`teardown-book-example.csv` is the book's example stack: $287 a month down to $90, $197 a month saved, about $2,364 a year. Four cells use the plugin's current names ([BOOK-FIXES.md](../../BOOK-FIXES.md)). The social scheduler and email tool rows only get drafts back: you still post and send, so don't cancel those two until you know where.
