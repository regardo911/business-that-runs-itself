# Client intake

- What tools and software subscriptions do they use now?
- What are their top three admin pains?
- Do they have an accountant or bookkeeper, and what's that relationship?
- How comfortable are they letting software act on their behalf?
- What are their hard red lines, the things they never want automated?
- Which single workflow would they most want working first?
