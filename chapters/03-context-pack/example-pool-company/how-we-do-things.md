# How We Do Things (the book's example business)

- We confirm every new job with a text the day before.
- We never start work over $500 without a signed estimate.
- Overdue invoices get one friendly reminder at 7 days, a firmer one at 14, a phone call at 30.
- Property managers always get a heads-up call before any price change.
