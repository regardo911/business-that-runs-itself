# Services and Pricing (the book's example business)

- Standard pool service: $150/month, weekly visits, includes chemicals and skimming.
- Green-to-clean recovery: $350 flat, for neglected pools.
- Filter replacement: parts at cost plus $90 labor.
- Equipment repair: $95/hour plus parts.
- Property managers: net-30 terms; everyone else pays when the invoice arrives.
