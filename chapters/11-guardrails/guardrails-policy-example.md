POOL COMPANY GUARDRAILS POLICY

Green — auto-allowed (read-only, run freely):
- /monday-brief, business-pulse ("Friday recap"), cash-flow-snapshot ("cash forecast")
- review-reputation ("pulse check on customers", the themes report only), content-strategy ("sales brief")
- Rule: these only summarize, so they run without approval.

Yellow — human-approved (I read and approve every one):
- Invoice reminders: I approve every message before it sends.
- New invoices: I check customer, line items, amount, due date.
- Customer replies: I approve every outbound reply (at least the first 30 days).
- Refunds under $[your number]: my approval required.
- Marketing posts: I approve before anything publishes or schedules.
- CRM updates: internal notes can be automatic; customer-facing follow-ups are approved.

Red — never automated (the irreversible and the human):
- Refunds over $[your number] without my review.
- Any message to [my biggest account] without my review.
- Any legal, tax, HR, or contract decision.
- Signing an estimate or a service agreement.
- Firing, disciplining, or hiring anyone.
- Changing prices for existing customers without my review.

Review rhythm:
- Re-check this policy monthly.
- Any workflow that makes a mistake moves back to stricter approval.
- New workflows get sorted green / yellow / red before they run.
